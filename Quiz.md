# Diary Activity Review: Quiz

Source: `course.md` (Feather course, sections 6–22). All answers below were confirmed correct.

---

## 1. Two-part activity, four videos

**Q:** An activity description covers two parts (e.g. "cooking lunch and having lunch"), and you have 4 videos: two show the cooking, two show the eating. Each pair fully covers its part on its own. How many videos should you select?

**A:** All four (1, 2, 3, 4). Choose **Yes**. Rule: select all qualifying videos, not the smallest set needed. (Sections 12, 15, FAQ 01)

---

## 2. Phone visible, game not

**Q:** The activity description says "I was playing games on my phone." A video shows the participant holding and looking at their phone, but you can't see the screen or hear any game audio. How should you answer, and what should the edited description say?

**A:** **Partly.** Phone use is confirmed, the game is not. Edited description: **"I was on my phone."** Explain that the person is shown on their phone, but it is impossible to confirm they were playing. Select all videos showing phone use. (Sections 13, 17)

---

## 3. Audio-only conversation

**Q:** The activity description is "talking to my sister", and a video has no visual of a second person but does have audio of a conversation. Should this video be included as evidence?

**A:** **Yes — audio evidence counts on its own; videos can confirm an activity through audio and/or video.**

Why not the other options:
- "No, audio alone can't be used" is wrong. Section 15 Example 3: an action audibly confirmed while the camera does not show it → include the video.
- "Only if the sister is seen briefly on screen" is wrong. The course never requires a visual of the other person. Section 11: "Visible" in the field label includes qualifying audio evidence. Section 14: a clip may establish an activity visually, audibly, or both.

Note: Yes vs Partly still depends on what the audio confirms. If "sister" cannot be established, choose Partly with a narrowed description (e.g. "talking to someone"). The video is included either way. (Sections 11, 14, 15, FAQ 03)

## 4. Emotions from audio

**Q:** The participant's face and body don't show any clear emotional cue in a video, but their tone of voice and laughter do. Can that audio be used when scoring their emotions?

**A:** **Yes.** Audio cues such as voice tone are observable cues. Choose Yes on the emotion question and score all three sliders. Do not infer internal state. (Section 19, FAQ 03)

---

## 5. Fixed scale for emotion scores

**Q:** When scoring Happy, Stressed, and Energized from 0-10, is there a fixed scale or set of reference anchors (e.g. "7 always means visible excitement") to follow?

**A:** **No fixed anchors.** Only scale given: 0 = no evidence of the emotion, 10 = very strong emotion. Rate each slider separately, based on the most obvious and persistent state across the activity, not one brief reaction. (Section 19)

---

## 6. Overlapping vs Adjacent

**Q:** What's the difference between the "Overlapping Videos" and "Adjacent Videos" tabs, and should you ever check Adjacent videos if an Overlapping video already seems to show the activity?

**A:** **Overlapping** = recordings inside the reported time window. **Adjacent** = recordings outside it. Start with Overlapping, but **always check Adjacent too**, even when Overlapping already confirms the activity. The time window may be wrong; either group can support Yes or Partly. (Sections 6, 8, 16)

---

## 7. Computer in a work-like setting

**Q:** A video shows the participant using a computer in what looks like a work setting, but there's no strict evidence of what they're actually doing. It's plausible they're working, but they could be doing something else. How should this be answered?

**A:** **Partly.** Computer use is confirmed; working cannot be verified. An office-looking room is context, not proof. Revised description: **"I was using my computer."** Plausible is not confirmed. (Sections 9, 13, 17)

---

## 8. Requirements when answering "No"

**Q:** If the activity description is judged "No" (not visible in the videos at all), what's required before submitting?

**A:** **Explicitly choose "None of these videos" — leaving it unanswered isn't allowed.** No revised description, no emotion scoring. Do not select nearby videos just because they are close in time. (Sections 11, 18, FAQ 02)

---

## 9. When to describe the visible part

**Q:** At which point in the process are you required to describe what part of the activity is visible in the video?

**A:** Right after answering **Partly** on "Is the Activity description clearly visible?". An extra question appears: "What part of the activity is visible?" Describe only the supported portion and why Partly was chosen. (Section 17)

---

## 10. Submitting with a blank required field

**Q:** You try to submit the task but left a required field blank. What happens?

**A:** **Submission is blocked.** The form shows validation errors pointing to the missing fields. Fix them, then submit. (Section 18)
